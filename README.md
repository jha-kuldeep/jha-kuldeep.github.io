### 🚚 Supply Chain Analysis

> **Stack:** &nbsp; `Python` &nbsp; `Pandas` &nbsp; `SQL` &nbsp; `Power BI` &nbsp; `Scikit-Learn` &nbsp; `Machine Learning`

<a href="https://github.com/jha-kuldeep/Supply_Chain_Analysis"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-View%20Repository-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white"/></a>

<br>

Analyzed **172,765+ supply chain transactions** to uncover delivery inefficiencies, revenue exposure, operational bottlenecks, and delay prediction opportunities.

<br>

**📌 Key Findings & Business Impact**

| Finding | Business Impact |
|:---|:---|
| **54.71% late delivery rate** identified | Exposed major operational inefficiencies affecting customer satisfaction |
| **$19.25M revenue** exposed to delay risk | Revealed critical financial exposure from delayed shipments |
| **100% delay rate** in First Class shipping | Critical logistics failure risking **$5.41M revenue** |
| **Tuned Random Forest — 86% accuracy** | Enables proactive late-delivery prediction before shipment |
| ML-driven intervention strategy proposed | Potential to prevent **~10% of delays → $1.93M revenue protected annually** |
| Regional bottleneck detection | Pinpointed **Central Africa & Southeast Asia** as high-risk logistics zones |

<br>

**🤖 Machine Learning Implementation**

Trained and evaluated 6 models: &nbsp; `Logistic Regression` &nbsp; `Decision Tree` &nbsp; `AdaBoost` &nbsp; `Gradient Boosting` &nbsp; `Random Forest` &nbsp; `Tuned Random Forest`

- ✅ **Best Model:** Tuned Random Forest &nbsp;|&nbsp; **86% Accuracy** &nbsp;·&nbsp; **85% F1 Score**
- 🔑 **Prediction Features:** Shipping Mode · Scheduled Shipment Days · Category · Customer Segment · Department · Region · Order Month · Order Hour
